Classification Pipeline

Files stream in from on-prem and cloud storage, get routed to a scanner matched to their type, are scored for sensitivity, and land in organized, severity-labeled storage. This is an illustrative target-state concept, not a rendering of any specific POC tier's actual detector stack.
